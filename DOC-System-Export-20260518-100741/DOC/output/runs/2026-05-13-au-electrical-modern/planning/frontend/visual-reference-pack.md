# Visual Reference Pack

## Hero Composition Specs
- Home: full-bleed real electrician or work-site image, left text stack, right trust rail, call CTA first, quote CTA second, rating chip above fold
- Services: centered editorial hero with category framing and compact trust strip under heading
- Residential: warm domestic setting with softer trust posture and homeowner reassurance chips
- Commercial: structured split with project or site image, metrics rail, and sector proof blocks
- Emergency Electrician: darker emergency-safe overlay with call-first rail, rapid-response reassurance, and visible safety guidance chip
- Switchboard Upgrades: technical image crop focused on board detail with clean explanatory panel
- EV Charger Installation: modern charger installation media, cleaner future-ready framing, but still trust-led and practical
- Smoke Alarm Compliance: bright, regulation-aware hero using checklist trust framing instead of emotional imagery
- Service Areas: map-adjacent or suburb-cluster visual with locality emphasis
- Projects: proof-led gallery hero with one standout completed project image and outcome tags
- Reviews: testimonial-led surface with rating emphasis and calmer imagery
- About: team-and-standards hero with authentic people imagery
- FAQ: utility intro with minimal media and strong clarity hierarchy
- Contact: contact-channel hero with phone, email, and hours grouped visibly
- Quote: form-first split with reassurance card, not a generic marketing hero

## Mobile Composition Specs
- Home: text and trust chips first, then dual CTA stack, then media crop; sticky call path preserved below
- Emergency: CTA stack appears before explanatory copy on mobile
- Quote: form top-loaded immediately after intro
- Commercial: metrics compress into horizontal chips above the fold
- Projects and Reviews: first proof item visible before deeper browsing

## Shared Surface Specs
- Header: transparent at top on Home only, solid on other routes, call CTA always visible on mobile
- Footer: trust-dense, contact-rich, hours and area cues above legal links
- Mobile bottom nav: icon plus label, five tabs max, active state must be obvious

## Asset Brief
- Required media types: licensed electricians on site, switchboard work, EV charger installs, lighting upgrades, commercial maintenance, vans, team portraits, compliant smoke alarm installs
- Avoid: stock-office smiles, fake customer-service headsets, abstract electric bolts, AI-polished pseudo-photography
- Required aspect ratios: 16:9 hero, 4:3 service proof, 3:2 projects, 1:1 avatars and badges
- Fallback policy: every key hero and proof slot needs a secondary asset or a gradient-backed fallback frame

## Banned Media Sources
- Generic stock collections without locality cues
- AI-generated electrician imagery posing as real work
- Remote imagery with broken hotlinks and no fallback
